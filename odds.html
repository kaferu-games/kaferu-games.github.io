ClickTac pack chances (확률형 아이템 확률 정보)

The sponsor's Courier can bring one pack of these kinds. These are the chances for every watch, as text you can search. Most packs start with 7 watches (one of each rarity and two more), and new watches join them after each Time Loop (an End of Time counts as one), so each pack shows one table per stage. In the game, the Service Desk shows your own chances, with your pack luck, guarantee, arrived watches and maxed watches included. The Sponsor's Lucky Draw is at the bottom of this page.

Lost pieces (not random). In each era from 3 to 12, the first pack of that era's own kind that you buy with Time while the era's lost piece is missing always contains that piece, in addition to its normal watch cards. The piece is a story item, not a watch, and it does not change the watch chances below. Until the piece is found, that pack sells at a lower price. Packs from the Courier never contain a lost piece.

How it works

  • Card 3 is always Uncommon or better. After 15 packs in a row without an Epic or Legendary, card 3 is Epic or better.
  • Pack luck multiplies the weights of Rare, Epic and Legendary by (1 + luck) before the chances are worked out; the game shows your exact chances.
  • A pack first rolls a rarity, then picks one of that pack's arrived watches of that rarity, each equally likely.
  • Watches you already have at max level are skipped while the pack has another arrived watch of that rarity below max level. If every arrived watch of the rolled rarity is at max level, the card moves to the nearest lower rarity that still has a watch to level up; only if there is none below, to the nearest higher one, but never up into Legendary (the card guarantees above still apply). So a Legendary comes only from its own chance. If no rarity the card can move to has a watch to level up, the card gives a maxed watch, which then adds nothing. The chances above are for a collection with no maxed watch.

Sponsor's Lucky Draw (one random power-up after a short ad)

These are the base chances, when every power-up can help you. In the game, a power-up that would do nothing for you right now is left out (for example Bench Reset when no ability is cooling down), and the chances of the others grow in proportion. The Service Desk's Chances page always shows your chances at that moment, and they are locked in when you tap Watch ad.
