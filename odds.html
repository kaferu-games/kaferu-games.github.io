How it works

  • Card 3 is always Uncommon or better. After 15 packs in a row without an Epic or Legendary, card 3 is Epic or better.
  • Pack luck multiplies the weights of Rare, Epic and Legendary by (1 + luck) before the chances are worked out; the game shows your exact chances.
  • A watch you already have at max level adds nothing.
