ClickTac Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information about you when you play ClickTac, a game for Android phones. We have tried to write it so that everyone, including young players, can follow it. If something is unclear, write to us (see "Who we are").

Short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no servers. We, the makers of ClickTac, do not receive, see or keep any information about you from the game.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • Ads are optional. You only see an ad when you tap a button to watch one.
  • Google shows the ads. To make ads possible, Google's ad software in the game connects to Google every time you open the game, even if you never watch an ad. Google then receives some technical information, such as your phone's IP address. Section 3 lists it all.
  • Every player gets the protections Google uses for children. Ads are never personalised. The game does not let Google's ad software use your phone's advertising ID. No other ad companies take part.
  • We never sell your information and never share it for targeted advertising.

1. What stays on your phone

These things are stored only on your phone, in the game's own folder. We never receive them.

  • Your save: your progress and settings, the time of your last save, and some technical clock readings that the game uses to work out how long you were away. It contains no name, no account and no device identifier.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, the game remembers when it planned a reminder and asks Android to show a notification on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.
  • Copy save to clipboard / Import save from clipboard:
    • "Copy save to clipboard" puts the text of your save on your phone's clipboard, so that you can paste it somewhere yourself. Other apps on your phone that can read the clipboard may see it.
    • "Import save from clipboard" reads the clipboard only when you tap that button.
  • Android backup: if backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may keep a copy of the game's data in your own Google account. Android can then put it back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. We cannot see that copy. You control it in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Backup) and in Google Drive (Storage → Backups).

2. What the game itself sends

Nothing. ClickTac has no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of its own. The Unity engine's own data collection ("diagnostics") is switched off. The only data that leaves your phone because of the game is described in sections 3 and 4.

3. Ads by Google AdMob

ClickTac contains Google's advertising software: the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform. It connects to Google when you open the game, when an ad is loaded and when you watch an ad. Google then receives:

  • your phone's IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region, city) can be estimated;
  • the app set ID: an identifier that is the same for all apps of one developer on your phone, used against fraud and to count ads;
  • app interactions: for example that the game started, that an ad was shown, and how much of a video ad was played;
  • diagnostic information: for example how fast the game and the ad software start, freezes and energy use;
  • device and app details: for example your phone model, Android version, language and the game's version.

The protections we switched on, for every player:

  • No personalised ads. Ads are never chosen from a profile of your interests, and there is no "remarketing".
  • No advertising ID. The game removes Android's permission for the advertising ID, and Google's ad software does not send it.
  • No other ad companies. Requests to outside ad vendors, ad-measurement pixels and third-party ad servers are switched off.
  • Family-friendly ads only. Ads must be suitable for all ages (rating "G").
  • Not sold or shared. Every ad request tells Google to use "restricted data processing". Under US state privacy laws this means Google may use the data only to provide the ad service to us, not to build profiles or sell it.

What Google does with this information. Google uses it to show you an ad, to limit how often you see the same ad, to count and measure ads, to keep its systems secure and to detect fraud. More information:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy (it also says which Google company is responsible where you live)

What we see. We do not receive any of this information. In our AdMob account we only see totals, for example how many ads were shown in each country and how much they earned.

4. Age checks by Google Play

Some places have laws that require app stores and app makers to check ages and parental approval, for example Texas and Louisiana in the United States.

  • Each time the game starts, before any ad is loaded, it asks Google Play on your phone whether it may check your age information.
  • Google may show its own message asking whether you want to share your age range (for example 13–15) with ClickTac. Whether this message appears depends on where you live and on your Google Play settings. You can say no. The game works the same either way.
  • If Google Play shares an answer, the game looks only at whether your account is supervised by a parent or guardian and whether the parent has approved the game and its latest changes. If a parent has not approved, the game shows a message and does not start. The game does not look at your age range.
  • The answer is used only on your phone and only to follow those laws. It is never stored, never sent to us or anyone else, and never used for ads. It is forgotten when you close the game.
  • You can change what Google Play shares in the Google Play app: Settings → Family → Age range sharing. Google Play's age checks are also explained in Google Play's help pages and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Watching ads is always optional. The whole game can be played without ever watching an ad.
  • Play offline. Without an internet connection nothing is sent. Ads are then not available.
  • Ad privacy choices: where a privacy law gives you a choice about ads, a button "Ad privacy choices" appears in the game under Settings → Privacy. You can change your choice there at any time.
  • Your phone's settings: you can delete or reset your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation for all apps (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

6. Why this information is used, and the legal reasons

  • Showing the ads that pay for the free game, counting them, and preventing fraud.
    • European Union, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free with ads that do not track you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We use the strongest protections Google offers (section 3). Some information is stored on your phone or read from it. This is only what is strictly needed to deliver an ad you asked for and to keep it secure.
    • Why the game does not ask for consent: players can be as young as 13, and in many countries young teenagers cannot give this consent themselves. So the game does not ask anyone for consent. It treats every player as possibly too young and switches on the protections above for everyone.
    • Brazil: legitimate interest (LGPD Article 7, IX), in the best interest of young players (LGPD Article 14).
    • Argentina: delivering the service you asked for (Ley 25.326, Article 5, paragraph 2, d).
  • Age checks (section 4): to comply with the laws that require them.
  • Answering your emails: to reply to you and help you. This is our legitimate interest, or what you asked us to do.

7. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • We keep nothing from the game, because we receive nothing.
  • Emails you send us: we keep them only while we deal with your question. We delete them at the latest 12 months after our last message to you. Our email service is Gmail, run by Google.
  • On your phone: your save stays until you remove it.
    • Settings → Reset save… starts a new game. A backup copy of the old game stays on the phone until the game saves again, usually within 30 seconds.
    • To remove everything at once, uninstall the game, or use Android's Settings → Apps → ClickTac → Storage → Clear storage.
    • If Android backup is on, also delete the ClickTac backup in your Google account. Otherwise the old game may come back when you reinstall.
  • Age check answers: never stored (section 4).
  • At Google: Google keeps advertising data for the periods described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. Google says there, for example, that it anonymises advertising data in server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months.

8. Security

  • Your save contains no personal information and stays in the game's own folder on your phone. On Android 10 and newer, other apps cannot read that folder. On older Android versions, an app that you have allowed to read your phone's storage could read it.
  • Everything Google's software sends is encrypted on the way (TLS).
  • We keep no player data on any server, so nothing about you can leak from us.

9. Information that crosses borders

Google processes data in several countries, including the United States. For data from the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google uses the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK and Swiss versions, and standard contractual clauses. See https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks.

We are in Argentina. The European Commission recognises that Argentina protects personal data adequately. The only personal information we ever receive is the emails you choose to send us.

10. Children and teenagers

  • ClickTac is meant for players 13 and older. It is not made for children under 13.
  • Even so, the game gives every player the protections that Google uses for children (section 3). It never asks for your age, name or email, and it has no chat and no way to share anything with other players.
  • United States (COPPA): the only information collected is the technical information in section 3, such as the IP address and the app set ID. It is used only to show non-personalised ads, to limit how often an ad repeats, to keep the service secure and to prevent fraud. It is not used to contact anyone, to build a profile or for behavioural advertising.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, or believe your child under 13 played the game, write to us. We will explain how to remove the game's data from the phone and how to ask Google about advertising data.

11. Your rights, and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal information:

  • Switzerland, and the EU, EEA and United Kingdom if you got the game there: access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection (including to our legitimate interest), data portability, and complaining to a data protection authority. Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
  • United States (state privacy laws): to know what is collected, access, correction, deletion, opting out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising (none of these happen in ClickTac), and not being treated worse for using your rights. If we say no to a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal". We answer the appeal within 45 days. If you disagree with the result, you can contact your state's Attorney General.
  • Argentina (Ley 25.326): access (free of charge every six months, or sooner if you show a legitimate interest), correction, updating and deletion. The authority is the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP): https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip
  • Brazil (LGPD): confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, information about sharing, and complaining to the ANPD: https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br

How to use them: write to kaferu.games@gmail.com. We answer within 10 days. If you ask us to correct or delete emails you sent us, we do it within 5 working days.

Because we hold no information about you from the game, we usually cannot look anything up or delete anything on our side. We will tell you exactly where to go:

  • Advertising data: Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com and https://myaccount.google.com, or your phone's ad settings.
  • Game data: section 7.
