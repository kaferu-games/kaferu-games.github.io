1. Who is responsible for your data

The person responsible for the processing described here (the "controller") is Nicolas Schcolnicov, publishing as Kaferu Games Studio, Buenos Aires, Argentina. Write to kaferu.games@gmail.com for any privacy question or request. Kaferu Games Studio is a private individual's trading name, not a company.

You can open this policy from the first screen of the game (the date-of-birth screen) and at any time from the game's Settings > Privacy policy button.

2. The short version

  • Moon Lap has no accounts, no chat, no online play, no cloud save, no leaderboards and no studio server. We do not run a backend that receives your data.
  • Your progress, settings and age group stay on your phone.
  • The game contains Google's ad library (Google AdMob) for one optional rewarded ad. The game requests an ad only after a player aged 13 or older taps "Watch ad: +1 warp fuel"; players under 13 never get that button. Section 3.3 explains what Google's library does, including what is not known about it.
  • When in-app purchases are available in the game, Google Play processes the payment.

3. What data is handled, why, and on what basis

3.1 Game progress and settings (stays on your device)

  • Data: your credits, parts, reputation, route, stars, perks, warp fuel, purchase flags, settings and your best-run ghost.
  • Why: to run the game and remember where you are.
  • Basis: needed to provide the game you asked to play (contract).
  • Leaves your device: no, by our design. The game turns off Android's cloud backup for its data. On some phones running Android 12 or later, app data may still be copied during a direct phone-to-phone transfer that you start yourself (source: https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/manifest/application-element, checked 2026-10-17).
  • Kept: until you uninstall the game or reset your progress. Uninstalling removes your save. When purchases are available, the bigger fuel tank and the Supporter pack can be restored with the Restore purchases button in the Shop after you reinstall; other progress cannot be restored.

3.2 Your age group (neutral age screen)

  • Data: when you first start, the game asks you to type your date of birth, with no preset answer. The game works out your age group — under 13 or 13 and over — and discards the date straight away. Only the age group is kept.
  • Why: to give under-13 players the limited mode (section 8: no ad button and no purchases), and to make the optional ad and purchases available only to players aged 13 and over.
  • Where: in the game's save on your device only; it is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Basis: our legitimate interest in routing under-13 players to the ad-free, purchase-free mode.
  • Kept: until you uninstall the game. It is not removed by the in-game "Reset progress" option.

3.3 Google's ad library and rewarded ads

The only ad in the game is an optional rewarded ad: on the out-of-fuel screen, a player aged 13 or older may tap "Watch ad: +1 warp fuel" to watch one ad and receive +1 warp fuel. The game never plays an ad automatically before a race. There are no banners, no interstitial ads and no other ad formats.

Google's ad library is part of the app for every player. Ads come from Google AdMob, through Google's Mobile Ads library, which is built into the game for all players, including players under 13. Android may start parts of this library when the app opens. The library also includes standard Android helper parts, such as one for scheduling background work, and other Google Play components. Google does not document whether the library sends anything at that point.

What the game itself does. The game requests an ad only after a player aged 13 or older taps "Watch ad: +1 warp fuel", and only if Google's consent check (section 3.4) allows ads. For players under 13 the game never requests an ad and never shows Google's consent form.

What Google says its library collects. According to Google's own disclosure, the library collects and shares with Google, automatically, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your IP address (which gives an approximate location),
  • product interactions (for example app launches, taps, video views),
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time, hang rate, energy use),
  • device and other identifiers, including the Google advertising ID and an app-set ID.

Google's disclosure does not say at which moment each item is sent. Google says this data is encrypted in transit. Source: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure (we read this page on 2026-10-17). Google decides what it does with this data under its own terms, as an independent party for its ad services; we do not receive the data, only whether the ad finished.

The game asks Google to treat ad requests as coming from a teen or unspecified age and to show only ads rated PG or lower (source: https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/targeting, checked 2026-10-17).

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your consent before the game requests any ad (section 3.4).
  • Kept: by Google, under Google's own retention rules; the studio keeps nothing.
  • Your choices: you can avoid ad requests by never tapping the ad button; street racing is free and always available. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

3.4 Your consent choices (13 and over)

For players aged 13 and over, the game runs Google's consent tool (Google's User Messaging Platform, part of the ads library) when it starts. The tool asks Google which consent message, if any, applies where you are, shows it if one does, and stores your consent choices on your device. Messages are currently set up for the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and for the US states whose privacy laws Google supports. Google does not document exactly what the tool sends to Google when it checks. The game does not run this check or show the consent form for players under 13.

Change or withdraw consent: open Settings > Privacy options in the game. This entry appears only where the consent tool reports that it is required. You can also remove ad personalisation in your Android settings.

3.5 Purchases (when available)

In-app purchases (a fuel refill, a bigger fuel tank, a Supporter pack) are not in the current version of the game. When they are available, they are sold and paid through Google Play (Google Play Billing), reached through Unity's purchasing library inside the game. The game then receives a purchase record — which product you bought and its order and purchase token — so it can give you the item and restore it. We do not see or store your card, payment or Google account details.

  • Basis: to carry out the purchase you requested (contract).
  • Under 13: the limited mode shows no purchase buttons.

3.6 Crash and performance data collected by Google Play

If your device has Android's usage and diagnostics sharing on, Google Play may collect crash and "not responding" data for the game and show it to us in aggregate. The game contains no crash-reporting library of its own. Google is responsible for that data.

3.7 Unity engine diagnostics

The game does not send Unity engine diagnostics. We removed Unity's analytics module, switched off Unity's analytics, ads, purchasing and cloud-diagnostics services, and the project is not connected to Unity Cloud; Unity's documentation says diagnostic data is disabled when a project is not connected to Unity Cloud (https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/Manual/diagnostics-settings.html, checked 2026-10-17).

3.8 Closed-test testers only

If you join our closed test, we receive the e-mail address you use for Google Play testing, through Google Play Console or Google Groups, only to run the test the store requires. Basis: your consent. Your address is removed from the tester list when the closed test ends, or sooner if you ask. Testers must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's permission.

5. International transfers

Google runs its services in many countries, so data handled by Google (sections 3.3 to 3.6 and 3.8) may be processed outside Argentina and outside your country, under Google's own terms.

6. How long we keep data

See each part of section 3. In short: the game keeps your save until you uninstall or reset it, and your age group until you uninstall; Google keeps ad and payment data under its own rules; the studio keeps no player records, only tester e-mails during the closed test.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley 25.326) you may ask to access the data a controller holds about you, and to have it corrected, updated or deleted. The law sets 10 calendar days to answer an access request and 5 business days to correct or delete (Arts. 14 and 16; source: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-25326-64790/texto, checked 2026-10-10). Players in other countries may have additional rights where they live, and we respect them.

  • To ask us: write to kaferu.games@gmail.com. Because the studio holds almost no data about you (section 2), the answer will often be that we hold nothing; we will tell you where the data you are asking about sits (for example, with Google).
  • To delete what is on your phone: uninstall the game (this removes your save and your age group; when purchases are available, the bigger fuel tank and the Supporter pack can be restored with the Restore purchases button in the Shop if you reinstall), or use the in-game "Reset progress" option (this removes your progress but keeps your age group).
  • For ad data held by Google: use Google's own tools, such as your Google account activity controls and resetting your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Complaints: you may contact Argentina's data-protection authority, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP) — https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales; datospersonales@aaip.gob.ar (checked 2026-10-16). If you live in the EU or UK, you may also contact your local authority.

8. Children

Moon Lap is made for players aged 13 and over. At first start, a neutral age screen asks for your date of birth with no preset answer and without naming any required age; only the age group is kept (section 3.2).

  • If you enter that you are under 13, the game opens a limited mode: you can play, there is no ad button and there are no purchase buttons, and fuel refills over time only. For these players the game never requests an ad and never shows Google's consent form. Google's ad library is still part of the app, and Android may start parts of it when the app opens; Google does not document whether it sends anything at that point (section 3.3). The studio itself receives nothing from these players; the age group stays on the device.
  • If you are 13 or over, the optional rewarded ad and, when available, purchases are open to you.
  • The Privacy policy button in Settings is available to every player, including in the limited mode.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has entered a false age, write to kaferu.games@gmail.com or uninstall the game; there is no account to close and nothing stored by the studio to delete.

9. Security

Your save is stored in the game's private storage on your phone, and ad and payment data travel to Google encrypted. We have no server to secure. No system is perfectly secure.
