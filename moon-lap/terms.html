8. Purchases (when available)

In-app purchases are not in the current version of the game. When they are available, they are paid with real money and processed by Google Play. The price shown on the buy button comes from Google Play in your currency and is the price you are charged. The planned items are:

  • Fuel refill (used up when granted): fills your warp fuel to your tank's maximum.
  • Bigger fuel tank (permanent): raises your tank from 5 to 8. It stays after season resets and game over.
  • Supporter pack (permanent, cosmetic only): a car paint, a boost flame colour and a supporter badge. It never gives credits, parts or fuel.

When purchases are available, the bigger fuel tank and the Supporter pack can be restored with the Restore purchases button in the Shop, for example after you reinstall.

No item is a random or "mystery" reward: you know what you get before you pay. Players in the under-13 limited mode see no purchase buttons.
